# Schaefer100 + Harvard-Oxford subcortical Parcellation

This parcellation file is named `atlas-SchaeferHarvOxf_nparc-114_space-MNI_res-2x2x2.nii.gz`, and is also available at 8 mm as `atlas-SchaeferHarvOxf_nparc-114_space-MNI_res-8x8x8.nii.gz`. It was previously named `HarvOxf-sub-Schaefer100-combined-2mm_4d.nii.gz` (and `_ds8` for the 8 mm file) in osl-ephys.

It is the Schaefer 100-parcel cortical parcellation ([Schaefer et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhx179), see [schaefer100.md](schaefer100.md)) followed by 14 subcortical structures from the Harvard-Oxford subcortical atlas distributed with FSL (thresholded at 50% and binarised): the thalamus, caudate, putamen, pallidum, hippocampus, amygdala and accumbens of each hemisphere. The coordinates below are from the 2 mm file.
